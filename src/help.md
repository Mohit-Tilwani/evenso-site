# Help

Quick answers for getting the most out of Evenso.

<div class="note"><p><strong>Please don't uninstall Evenso to fix a problem.</strong> Everything you've entered lives only on your phone, so uninstalling deletes it. Install updates over the top, and make a backup first if you ever do need to remove it.</p></div>

## Backing up your data

- **Android backup.** If backup is on in your phone's settings, Android can include Evenso in your device backup, so it comes back when you set up a new phone with the same Google account.
- **Automatic copies.** In Evenso, go to **Settings → Backup and your data**, pick **Weekly** or **Monthly** and choose a folder (for example one that syncs to your cloud drive). Evenso keeps the latest four copies there.
- **Export now.** On the same page, **Export backup** saves a file wherever you choose, and **Restore from backup** brings one back.

## Widgets

Long-press an empty spot on your home screen, tap **Widgets**, find **Evenso** and drag one out.

- **Today** shows your habit rings and your top tasks. Tick a task right on the widget; tap a ring to open the app. When a check-in is due, it shows a button to start it. The small size shows rings only.
- **Ticker** shows what's coming up this week: birthdays, anniversaries, holidays, gifts to buy and your own events. Choose **one line** (everything in turn) or **two lines** (today's holiday on top, the week underneath). Tap an item to open it.

Widgets follow your phone's light or dark mode.

## The daily rhythm

- **Morning check-in:** how you slept, plus energy, stress and mood. It takes under a minute.
- **End-of-day review:** one quick yes or no for each habit, then a short wrap-up. If you miss it, Evenso asks the next time you open it.
- Missed a day? That's fine. Streaks are there to cheer you on, not to punish you.

## Reminders not showing up?

Some phones stop apps in the background to save battery. If reminders or alarms arrive late or not at all:

- Allow notifications for Evenso in your phone's settings.
- For sleep alarms, allow **Alarms & reminders** when Evenso asks.
- Set Evenso's battery use to **Unrestricted** (on Samsung: Settings → Apps → Evenso → Battery).

## Optional extras

In **Settings → Optional extras** you can use Evenso as your home screen, or turn on a lock-screen glance of today's events. Both are off unless you turn them on.

## Something else?

Use **Settings → Send feedback** in the app, or email [hello@evenso.me](mailto:hello@evenso.me). We read every email.
