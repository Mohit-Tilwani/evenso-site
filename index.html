Questions

Is Evenso a habit tracker?

It's a habit builder. Tell it what you're working towards (better sleep, less stress, sharper focus) and it suggests a few small daily habits to start with. A short check-in each morning and a wrap-up each night keep you honest. Streaks are there to cheer you on; a missed day doesn't wipe out your progress.

Does it remind me about birthdays and gifts in advance?

Yes. Birthdays, anniversaries and holidays come with a heads-up days or weeks ahead, depending on how close the person is, plus a reminder to buy the gift in time.

Do I need an account? Where is my data?

No account. Everything stays on your phone. There are no ads and no tracking; you can back up with Android backup or to a folder you choose.

Is it free?

Yes, Evenso is free. Optional extras may come later as one-time purchases.

Does it have home-screen widgets?

Yes: your rings and today's tasks, and a ticker for what's coming up this week.
