Stay in touch

People who matter, in circles

Put each person in a circle: Core, Inner, Middle or Outer. Closer circles get earlier heads-ups and more nudges.

  • A nudge when it's been a while. "You haven't spoken to Kai in 3 weeks."
  • Birthdays and anniversaries, with the message ready. One tap opens WhatsApp or SMS with the wish written.
  • Gift heads-up weeks ahead. "Maya turns 32 in 14 days. Buy the gift this week."
  • Sent in time. If it has to travel, a reminder to send it a few days before.

Tasks done

Tasks, closed out

No endless someday list. Every task gets a day, and the late ones come first.

  • Today, with what's next beside it. Urgent things and the next 7 days sit next to Today.
  • Not today? One tap to Move. Pick a new day and get on with it.
  • Nothing slips. The night wrap-up catches anything still open.
  • A focus timer that asks what you're finishing. Pick the task, start the timer, tick it off when it rings.

Questions

Is Evenso a habit tracker?

It's a habit builder. Tell it what you're working towards (better sleep, less stress, sharper focus) and it suggests a few small daily habits to start with. A short check-in each morning and a wrap-up each night keep you honest. Streaks are there to cheer you on; a missed day doesn't wipe out your progress.

Does it remind me about birthdays and gifts in advance?

Yes. Birthdays, anniversaries and holidays come with a heads-up days or weeks ahead, depending on how close the person is, plus a reminder to buy the gift, and to send it in time.

Do I need an account? Where is my data?

No account. Everything stays on your phone. There are no ads and no tracking; you can back up with Android backup or to a folder you choose.

Is it free?

Yes, Evenso is free. Optional extras may come later as one-time purchases.

Does it have home-screen widgets?

Yes: your rings and today's tasks, and a ticker for what's coming up this week.
